**Remotely Operated Vehicle (ROV)** — End-to-end hardware-software co-design of an underwater robot achieving 100% 3D-printed fabrication and comprehensive hull structure simulation.

### Physical Prototyping & Sim-to-Real Design

Led the complete design-to-deployment pipeline: CAD modeling → 3D printing → electronics integration → software deployment. Achieved full **3D-printed fabrication** of the pressure hull and structural components, with comprehensive **CFD/FEA simulation** bridging the sim-to-real gap for embodied robotic systems.

### Edge Perception

Deployed real-time **YOLO object detection on Raspberry Pi**, enabling low-latency visual awareness for autonomous underwater interaction.
